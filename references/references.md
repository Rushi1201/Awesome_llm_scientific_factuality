# Curated Research Papers

Minimum required: 20. All entries include an authoritative paper or persistent-identifier link.

## Surveys and Foundations

### 1. TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods
- **Authors:** Stephanie Lin; Jacob Hilton; Owain Evans
- **Year/Venue:** 2022, ACL
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.229/
- **Relevance:** General-purpose truthfulness benchmark built around questions involving common misconceptions.

### 2. Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation
- **Authors:** Ziwei Ji; Nayeon Lee; Rita Frieske; et al.
- **Year/Venue:** 2023, ACM Computing Surveys
- **Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3571730
- **Relevance:** Broad survey establishing terminology and evaluation approaches for hallucination.

### 3. Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks
- **Authors:** Patrick Lewis; Ethan Perez; Aleksandara Piktus; et al.
- **Year/Venue:** 2020, NeurIPS
- **Paper:** https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/hash/6b493230205f780e1bc26945df7481e5-Abstract.html
- **Relevance:** Foundational retrieval-augmented generation approach relevant to grounding model outputs.

## Scientific and Biomedical Benchmarks

### 4. Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims
- **Authors:** David Wadden; Shanchuan Lin; Kyle Lo; et al.
- **Year/Venue:** 2020, EMNLP
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/
- **Relevance:** Introduces SciFact for scientific claim verification against research abstracts.

### 5. PubMedQA: A Dataset for Biomedical Research Question Answering
- **Authors:** Qiao Jin; Bhuwan Dhingra; Zhengping Liu; William Cohen; Xinghua Lu
- **Year/Venue:** 2019, EMNLP-IJCNLP
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/D19-1259/

### 6. Towards Expert-Level Medical Question Answering with Large Language Models
- **Authors:** Karan Singhal; Tao Tu; Juraj Gottweis; et al.

### 8. FELM: Benchmarking Factuality Evaluation of Large Language Models

## Hallucination Detection and Mitigation
